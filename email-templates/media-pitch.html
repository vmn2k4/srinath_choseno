One tap: your reps, your races, your candidates

Choseno reads your location and works out your electoral boundaries. It then shows your current representatives, the races happening in your area, and who is running in each one. No postal-code lookups. It is the first service we know of that does this.

What we're launching now: a social network for local issues

When people join, they are grouped by their municipality, school district and other local boundaries. A parent can raise a problem at their school district. A resident can flag a pothole or a zoning change. Neighbours discuss it in one place.

Our goal is to take those issues to the right department and get them resolved. We want to be the bridge in democracy between residents and the people who decide.

Why it isn't Facebook or X

  • No real names. There are no friends, family or profiles attached. People post under anonymous, rotating IDs, so they can say what they actually think without fear of repercussions.
  • No influencers. Nobody builds a following here. A post is judged on what it says.
  • Verified local. Posts are tied to real electoral boundaries, so the conversation comes from people who live there.
  • Candidates can answer directly. Future leaders can connect with their district without a campaign budget.
