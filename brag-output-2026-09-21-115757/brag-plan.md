# Brag Plan: Choseno

## What is this app?

Choseno is a civic engagement platform that connects voters directly with their elected officials through interactive maps, news feeds, and public engagement walls — making political accountability local, personal, and actionable.

## The angle

Not "I voted once." **"You can actually talk to them."** 

The hook is the direct line between voter and elected official. The video is about agency — you find YOUR representative (not just any politician), and you can post questions, ratings, and engagement directly to them. It's accountability made visible.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

**"Your representatives are here."**

A map zooms in tight to a single district. A pin drops. Then: cut to a name and face. The moment of discovery — you know exactly who represents you, and they're not abstract anymore.

*Timing: 2.5s*

## Key moments (the middle)

1. **The interactive map lights up** — a district boundary highlights, showing geographic clarity. Other boundaries fade. This is the visual centerpiece: the geography of power becomes visible.

2. **A politician's wall appears** — a profile card with their name, party, and a small feed of posts below. It's a real politician, with a real public presence. This is the product doing the work.

3. **A voter's post lands on the wall** — a new post appears with a question or comment. The wall is interactive, not ceremonial. The voter has a voice.

## Outro / punchline

**"Know your representatives. Engage with them. Make politics local."**

A montage of 3-4 different politician walls/districts scrolling past, showing the scale and diversity of the platform. Final frame: Choseno logo with tagline.

## User flow worth showing

Yes — the three-beat engagement flow:

1. **Entry**: Voter lands on the app, searches for their district (or location is detected). The "Find My District" interactive map is the centerpiece.
2. **Discovery**: The map reveals which politicians represent that district. A politician's wall/profile appears — showing their public posts, ratings, and constituent engagement.
3. **Action**: Voter writes a post or question directly on the politician's wall. The post appears live, creating a permanent public record of engagement.

This is the strongest material — it shows the product *working*, not just describing itself. The map interaction + wall UI + posted engagement are the three visuals that make Choseno unique.

## Tone

- Preset: **polished**
- Creative direction: Civic. Serious about democracy. Not cynical, not idealistic — pragmatic. This is infrastructure, and it matters.
- Interpretation: Slower pace, longer holds, confident restraint. The video doesn't *try* to be exciting — the idea itself is exciting. Typography is clean and readable. Every visual moment earns its place. No generic motion graphics.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 18 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)

Based on Choseno's site aesthetic:

- **Background**: Clean white or very light gray (#FAFAFA or #FFFFFF) — civic, professional, trustworthy.
- **Accent**: Deep blue (#1e40af or similar) — used for buttons, highlights, and map interactions. Trust and stability.
- **Text**: Dark gray/near-black (#1F2937) for body, same blue for CTAs and highlights.
- **Display font**: Modern sans-serif (look for font used in Choseno's headers — likely system stack or geometric sans like Inter, Poppins)
- **Body font**: Clean sans-serif (system font stack for legibility)
- **Strongest visual element**: The interactive map with district boundaries and politician pins. This is the hero. The politician walls/profiles are the secondary visual.

## Share copy (draft)

**"Direct democracy isn't a dream — it's a map, a profile, and a post. Choseno connects you to your representatives. Your voice matters."**

*Alternative shorter:* **"Talk to your representatives. Choseno makes it real."**

## Audio direction

- **Role**: Warm, professional support. The music should feel like forward momentum — not cinematic, not urgent, but purposeful.
- **Music**: Upbeat but not frantic. Think "startup optimism" meets "civic infrastructure." Positive, clean, mid-tempo (around 90-110 BPM). A bed that feels like progress without hype.
- **Music treatment**: Fade in gently under the hook. Builds slightly as the map reveals. Holds steady through the wall/engagement moments. Slight swell at the outro montage. Fade out clean with the logo reveal.
- **SFX posture**: Sparse, professional restraint. This isn't a game video.
  - Map interactions: subtle pop/whoosh as district highlights and pins drop (very light, no aggression)
  - Card reveal/wall appearance: soft transition sound
  - Post appearing: gentle success/confirm chime (like a notification arriving)
  - No typing sounds — too cute for this tone
- **Audio-coupled moments**: 
  - Map zoom + district highlight should sync to a music beat or SFX cue
  - Wall card appearance pairs with a subtle reveal sound
  - Post landing should sync to a satisfying but understated chime
- **Restraint rule**: No whooshes, no impacts, no aggressive transitions. Keep audio elegant and purposeful. The idea is powerful; audio supports it, doesn't oversell it.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Hook: "Your Representatives Are Here" — 2.5s

A map fills the screen. Multiple district boundaries are visible in light gray. A zoom animation tightens in on one district. That district's boundary highlights in deep blue. A pin icon drops at the center.

Over the final frame of the pin landing, a politician's name and photo appear in the lower third: "Sarah Chen • MP, Vancouver West"

**Sequential/interaction**: Yes — zoom in on map, district highlight, pin drop. Natural geographic focus.

**Audio intent**: A sense of arrival. The map zoom should feel smooth and confident, not rushed.

**Audio-coupled idea**: If using a subtle SFX, a light pop or chime as the pin lands. Music beat aligns with the pin drop.

**Music**: Warm, upbeat, begins fading in at the zoom.

**Transition mood**: Smooth crossfade → Scene 2

---

### Scene 2 — The Wall: "Engage Directly" — 3.5s

The politician's wall/profile appears — a clean card or panel showing:
- Politician name, party, a photo
- A feed of recent posts/comments below
- A text input field at the bottom (faint, like "Write a comment...")

The scene shows the wall is *active* — you can see existing constituent posts, ratings, or engagement already on the wall.

**Sequential/interaction**: Optional — 2-3 small past posts could fade in one by one to show activity, OR the wall just appears as a static-but-populated state. Go with the latter for polished tone (one clean reveal, not sequential noise).

**Audio intent**: Clarity and presence. The wall appearing should feel like a door opening, not a surprise.

**Audio-coupled idea**: Subtle card reveal sound as the wall appears. No animation necessary, but sound marks the moment.

**Music**: Music continues steadily. Same bed. Confident.

**Transition mood**: Soft crossfade → Scene 3

---

### Scene 3 — The Action: "Your Voice Matters" — 3s

A cursor (or simulated fingertap) appears on the text field. The message types out:

**"Will you support the new transit proposal?"**

As the text appears, it lands in the feed as a new post. The post is styled as a voter's contribution — clean, readable, live on the wall.

**Sequential/interaction**: Yes — text appears character by character (fast, ~0.5s total for typing). Then the post lands in the wall feed.

**Audio intent**: Purposeful. The action of writing and posting should feel significant but not heavy.

**Audio-coupled idea**: 
- Subtle keyboard/typing sounds are optional (can be omitted for polished restraint; if included, very quiet).
- A soft success/confirm chime when the post lands in the feed.

**Music**: Music holds steady or builds very slightly as the post lands. This is the climax moment.

**Transition mood**: Smooth transition → Scene 4

---

### Scene 4 — The Scale: "Politics Made Local" — 3.5s

A montage of 3-4 different politician walls/profiles scrolling or transitioning past the screen, each showing:
- A different politician (different name, party, district)
- A live wall with constituent posts/engagement

This shows the breadth and scale of Choseno without losing the intimacy. Each wall is a real community engagement space.

**Sequential/interaction**: Card-by-card wipe or crossfade. Each new politician wall appears as the previous one exits. No user interaction — just showing the ecosystem.

**Audio intent**: A sense of movement and scale. Music can build slightly here.

**Audio-coupled idea**: Each card transition could sync to a subtle sound or music beat, but keep it minimal. Avoid rapid-fire clicks.

**Music**: Music swells or maintains energy. The bed continues or builds toward outro.

**Transition mood**: Smooth/hard cut → Scene 5 (Final frame)

---

### Scene 5 — The Outro: "Know Your Representatives" — 2s

Full fade to Choseno logo on clean background (white or light gray). 

Beneath: **"Choseno: Know Your Representatives. Engage. Make Politics Local."**

Small URL or CTA below (e.g., "Visit Choseno.com" or "Try now").

**Sequential/interaction**: None — just logo reveal with text.

**Audio intent**: Resolution and confidence. Music should resolve cleanly.

**Audio-coupled idea**: None — just the music landing on a final note or beat.

**Music**: Music resolves to a final note/swell. Feels complete.

**Transition mood**: Fade to black or hold on logo.

## Music mood for this video:

**Upbeat, purposeful, civic forward-momentum** — not cynical, not hype, but optimistic and grounded. Think "startup infrastructure" meets "democracy toolkit." Clean, professional, mid-tempo (90–110 BPM).

## Audio summary:

A clean, upbeat music bed supports the entire video, with subtle SFX marking key moments (map pin drop, wall reveal, post success chime). No aggressive sounds or excessive motion graphics audio. The video's power comes from the idea and the product, not from audio fireworks.

---

## Music cue guidance

- **Track personality**: Upbeat, professional, slightly optimistic. Around 95–110 BPM works well.
- **Strong cue moments to target**:
  - ~1s (as the map zooms and district highlights) — an uplifting beat or swell
  - ~7.5s (as the post lands on the wall) — a satisfying resolution or beat drop
  - ~11s (start of the montage scaling) — energy lift for the ecosystem reveal
- **Beat grid for sequential reveals**: The wall montage in Scene 4 can land each politician card on a beat or half-beat (~0.5s apart) if the music has clear rhythm.
- **Cue availability**: To be detected at composition time via beat analysis if using a custom track, or read from preset if using a bundled Hyperframes track.

---

## Restraint reminders (polished tone)

- No swooshes, impacts, or aggressive SFX
- Typing sound is optional; if included, very subtle
- Music does not need to be loud or complex — clean and purposeful wins
- Every visual moment should feel earned and necessary
- The map interaction and wall engagement are the heroes; everything else supports them
