## Tips for Sharing

1. **Use municipality-specific hashtags** (#VancouverVotes, #SurreyElects, etc.)
2. **Post during peak engagement times** (evenings 6-9 PM, weekend mornings)
3. **Pair with candidate photos/bios** when you feature specific individuals
4. **Ask follow-up questions**: "What issue matters most to you in your municipality?"
5. **Encourage voter registration**: Link to BC Elections voter registration pages
6. **Cross-post variations**: Use different angles (housing, schools, environment) for different followers
