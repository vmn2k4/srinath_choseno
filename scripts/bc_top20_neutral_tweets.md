## 📋 Tweet Customization Guide

### Tone Options for Variation
- **Civic Participation** (used above): "Your vote counts," "Vote informed," "Learn about candidates"
- **Issue-Focused**: Swap emoji/opening for specific focus: "Housing crisis," "Transit funding," "School budgets"
- **Urgency**: "Elections coming soon," "Don't miss the deadline," "Registration is open"
- **Engagement**: Replace with questions: "What's YOUR top local priority?" "Who will you vote for for [role]?"

### How to Make These More Viral

1. **Add election dates**: "Langley City votes on [DATE]"
2. **Reference local issues**: "Surrey's housing shortage—where do candidates stand?"
3. **Pair with candidate photos**: When featuring specific races, add images
4. **Use municipality-specific hashtags**: #VancouverVotes #SurreyChooses #BurnabyCommunity
5. **Tag local media/influencers**: "@SurreyLeader what issues matter most to voters?"
6. **Ask questions to spark replies**: "What's your top issue for your mayor?"
7. **Thread format**: Post 3-5 tweets in a row hitting different angles (housing, transit, schools)

### Performance Boosters

- **Post timing**: Evenings (6-9 PM) and Saturday mornings get highest engagement
- **Frequency**: 1-2 per day, spaced out so they don't compete
- **Pairings**: Post complementary angles on same day (e.g., housing + council race)
- **Engagement**: Like/reply to candidate posts and local news about these races
- **Hashtags to add**:
  - #BCElections2026
  - #Vote2026
  - #LocalElections
  - [Municipality]Votes
  - [Municipality]Elects
  - [Municipality]Community

### For Even More Reach

**Create "comparison" tweets** between races:
- "5 Vancouver mayoral candidates. 3 visions for housing. 1 vote. Learn the differences."
- "School trustee races in BC: Why do they matter? [link to Saanich race] [link to Surrey race]"

**Create "deadline" tweets** (timing is key):
- "Nomination deadline is [DATE]. Want to run? Here's how. Here's who's running: [link]"

**Create "debate alert" tweets**:
- "Surrey council candidates debate TONIGHT at [venue]. Live coverage here: [link]"
