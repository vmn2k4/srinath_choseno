✅ POST FREQUENCY: 2-3 per day (October 1-17)
✅ BEST TIMES: 6-9 PM weeknights, 9 AM-1 PM weekends
✅ HASHTAGS: Always use #BCPoli, #VotingMatters, #Oct17, [RidingName]
✅ ENGAGEMENT: Reply to comments, encourage shares
✅ PIN: Pin the first 5 posts to top of your page during election month
✅ BOOST: Consider a small ad budget ($10-50/post) for ridings with most engagement

VARIATIONS:
- Ask questions: "What's your #1 priority for BC?"
- Poll format: "Vote for the issue YOU care most about"
- Local hooks: Reference local news, recent events
- Encourage sharing: "Tag someone who should vote!"
